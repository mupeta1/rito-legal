# Rito Data Deletion Instructions Rito respects users' requests to delete their personal information. ## How to request deletion To request deletion of your Rito account and associated personal information, contact the Rito team through the official contact channel provided by Rito. Please include the phone number associated with your account and state that you are requesting data deletion. Do not include passwords or other sensitive credentials. ## What happens next We will review your request, verify your identity where necessary, and explain any additional information required to process it. Where applicable, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, subject to legal obligations, legitimate recordkeeping requirements, and the resolution of outstanding disputes. ## Contact For help with a data deletion request, contact the Rito team through its official support channel. These instructions should be updated with Rito's actual support contact details before public launch.
