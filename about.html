ABOUT RITO

Making everyday journeys more connected.

Rito Transport Services Limited is developing transport services around a simple idea: make it easier for people travelling in the same direction to connect with a ride.

Our approach

Rito's focus is shared transport, supported by private-ride options. We aim to build services that are practical for passengers and drivers, with clear journey details and a focus on reliability.

Our services

Rito Share is our shared-journey concept. Private rides are a separate option. Availability and operating areas may change as the service develops, so please contact us to confirm what is currently available.

Our commitment

We aim to communicate clearly about service availability and handle personal information in accordance with our published privacy policy.

Rito is being developed and operated by Goglyn Tech Limited to make everyday transport more connected, convenient and accessible. Rito is a brand of Goglyn Tech Limited, which is responsible for the service.
