# Rito Privacy Policy **Effective date:** 20 October 2026 Rito is a transport and logistics service operated under the Rito brand. ## Information we collect Depending on how you use Rito, we may collect your name, phone number, account details, pickup and destination locations, ride requests, driver and passenger communications, and trip-related information. ## How we use information We use personal information to register users, coordinate rides, connect passengers with drivers, support trip tracking, communicate about bookings, improve our services, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. ## Location information When needed for a ride, Rito may process pickup locations, destinations, and driver location updates to coordinate and monitor trips. Location information should be used only for legitimate service purposes. ## Sharing information We may share relevant trip information between passengers and drivers to arrange and complete rides. We may also use service providers that support hosting, communications, and platform operations. We do not sell personal information as a business practice. ## Data security and retention We use reasonable measures to protect personal information. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and legitimate operational needs. ## Your choices and requests You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. See our Data Deletion Instructions page for how to make a request. ## Contact For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the Rito team through the official contact channel provided by Rito. This policy should be updated as Rito's actual data practices and contact details are finalized.
